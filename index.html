THE CEYLON LEGACY

A gemstone tradition shaped by island, earth & time.

Sri Lanka has been renowned for gemstones for centuries. Its name, Ceylon, remains closely associated with exceptional sapphires and a remarkable variety of coloured stones.

At Ceylon Gems, every stone deserves clear information and thoughtful presentation—from its colour and cut to its carat weight and treatment status.

GEM GUIDE

Buy with knowledge.

01

Colour

Colour is one of the most important factors in evaluating a coloured gemstone. Look for hue, tone and saturation.

02

Cut & clarity

A well-executed cut can improve a stone's brilliance and visual appeal while clarity varies naturally by gem type.

03

Treatment

Always ask whether a gemstone has been heated or otherwise treated, and request documentation where appropriate.
