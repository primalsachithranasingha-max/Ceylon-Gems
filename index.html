Connoisseur Knowledge

The 4Cs of Ceylon Sapphires

Understanding the gemological standards that define investment-grade natural unheated corundums.

1. Hue & Saturation (Color)

The foremost valuation factor. Royal Blue features rich vivid saturation without dark extinction; Padparadscha requires a delicate 50/50 pink-orange balance; Cornflower radiates velvety daylight glow.

2. Crystal Clarity & Silk

Ceylon corundums are Type II gemstones. Microscopic rutile silk needles confirm natural provenance and create the iconic velvety luminescence prized over glassy synthetic stones.

3. Master Lapidary Cut

Unlike commercial cuts that retain weight at the cost of brilliance, Ceylon Gems SL gems are precision-faceted with strict optical symmetry to eliminate windowing and maximize light return.

4. Carat & Unheated Rarity

Natural unheated Ceylon sapphires over 3 carats represent less than 0.5% of total mine output. Their valuation escalates exponentially per carat weight on the global auction market.

Client Inquiries & Assurance

Are all gemstones 100% natural and ethically mined from Sri Lanka?

Yes. Every gemstone in our collection is ethically recovered from historical artisanal alluvial gravels in Ratnapura, Elahera, and Balangoda without heavy industrial devastation. Each piece undergoes exhaustive gemological testing by GIA, GRS, or Gübelin to guarantee natural origin and verify heat treatment status.

How does insured international shipping and customs clearance work?

We partner with premier high-value secure transit services (Ferrari Express, Malca-Amit, Brinks). Shipments are 100% fully insured from our vault to your door or private bank vault, complete with NGJA export documentation and customs clearance coordination.

Can I commission a completely custom bespoke ring with a loose gem?

Absolutely. You can select any loose certified gemstone from our catalog, configure setting styles and metals in our online Bespoke Atelier, or collaborate directly with our master goldsmiths on a one-of-a-kind hand-forged creation.
